      ## Privacy Policy

      WibbleJelly Games (the trading name of WibbleJelly Ltd.) does not store any personal or financial details supplied to purchase goods from our website or to hire any of our products. WibbleJelly Games hate spam, so we will never sell your email address or details to a third party or send you emails on behalf of a third party.

      ## Cookies

      Cookies are small text files of information that are stored by a website on a user’s computer. Some cookies are essential for the correct operation of the website. Other cookies, whilst not essential, assist in the correct functioning of the website and enhance the user’s experience. More information about cookies in general can be found at [www.allaboutcookies.org](http://www.allaboutcookies.org/).

      We use a session cookie to ensure that the website functions correctly, this cookie is automatically destroyed when you close down your browser or turn off your computer.

      We also use persistent cookies to record when various popup messages have been displayed on the website.

      **If you would have any questions about our Privacy Policy or Cookies, please [contact us](/contact/).**
