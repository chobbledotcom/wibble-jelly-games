      # About Us

      WibbleJelly Games are a family run business based in Yorkshire, we design and manufacture innovative games for the Activities Hire Industry. Our original games are compact, easy to set up and economical to run, bringing additional revenue opportunities to your business. Our products suit any party occasion guaranteeing fun for any age group.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      We have our own workshop equipped with a Laser Cutter, CNC Router, 3D Printer, Lathe and a Pick & Place Machine for making PCBs. We have a background in Electronics and Software Engineering. We pride ourselves on designing and building all of the games we sell, we will always support any game we sell. We do not import completed games from the Far East and pass them off as our own!

      WibbleJelly Games was founded by Nick Forbes in 2015 based on nearly 10 years of experience designing and running games for school fetes and large Scout camps and Jamborees. I didn’t want to have to run the hoopla or help on the burger stand so challenged myself to build bigger and better games for the fetes, visit [www.fetefun.co.uk](http://www.fetefun.co.uk/) to see a selection of the games I built.

      Being heavily involved in Scouting I agreed to run an activity zone at the 2009 Windsor International Scout And Guide Camp (WINGS) where I was responsible for providing activities to keep 500 young people entertained over a 3 hour period during 10 sessions over 5 days. This was followed in 2014 by accepting the position of Head Of Onsite Activities for WINGS and now being responsible for keeping 6000 young people entertained over 6 activity zones.

      A lot of the activities that I designed for the activity zones formed the bases of the activities we hire out. We aim to provide something different at camp and now find that the young people recognise us from previous camp and flock to our activities.

      We still use our regular attendance at Scout camps and Jamborees to test new games. If a new game can survive being played hard and pounded over a week by 6000 young people it is ready to go on sale!
