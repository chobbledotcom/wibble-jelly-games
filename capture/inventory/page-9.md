# Spot On!

[figure.wp-block-media-text__media]
![Spot On! Game](https://wibblejellygames.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/SpotOn-680x1024.png) width=680 height=1024 class=wp-image-11 size-full srcset=https://wibblejellygames.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/SpotOn-680x1024.png 680w, https://wibblejellygames.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/SpotOn-199x300.png 199w, https://wibblejellygames.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/SpotOn-768x1156.png 768w, https://wibblejellygames.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/SpotOn.png 800w
Can you stop the fast moving light exactly on the centre spot? Everyone loves to press the Big Red Button and the better you are the faster it goes!
With sound effects and a catchy attention grabbing tune.
This game is great at any fun day, corporate event, wedding, youth club, birthday party or any other event you may be having.
The legs are detachable and pack away into the flightcase lid along with the Big Red Button and its base. Everything is contained and won’t get lost.
Game throughput is high as each game lasts 60 seconds with players coming back time and time again as they try and gain a High Score to climb up the Leader Board.
The “Spot On!” branding and the “High Score” Leader Board are both attached using velcro so can easily be swapped out for corporate branding at trade shows and corporate events. The Leader Board comes with magnetic white board strips so it is easy to add a player when they climb up the leader board.

#### ***Power Requirements:***
Input Voltage: 90 – 264 VAC
Input Power: < 49 Watts
Fuse – Mains Lead: 3 Amp
Spot On! has an IEC socket mounted on the back and a mains lead is provided with a UK style mains plug. Other types of mains leads can be supplied on request. There is also a volume control and a socket for the detachable Big Red Button in recessed panels on the back.
