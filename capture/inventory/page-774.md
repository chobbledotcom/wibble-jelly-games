# Spot On! – Challenge

[figure.wp-block-media-text__media]
![](https://wibblejellygames.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/m_SpotOnChallenge-1-614x1024.png) width=614 height=1024 class=wp-image-776 size-full srcset=https://wibblejellygames.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/m_SpotOnChallenge-1-614x1024.png 614w, https://wibblejellygames.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/m_SpotOnChallenge-1-180x300.png 180w, https://wibblejellygames.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/m_SpotOnChallenge-1.png 720w
Can you stop the fast moving light exactly on the centre spot? Everyone loves a race and the better you are the faster it goes!
With sound effects and a catchy attention grabbing tune.
This game is great at any fun day, corporate event, wedding, youth club, birthday party or any other event you may be having.
The legs are detachable and pack away into the flightcase lid along with the buttons and leads. Everything is contained and won’t get lost.
Game throughput is high as the game is 2 player and each game lasts 45 seconds with players coming back time and time again as they try to beat each other and gain a High Score.

#### ***Power Requirements:***
Input Voltage: 90 – 264 VAC
Input Power: < 49 Watts
Fuse – Mains Lead: 3 Amp
Spot On! – Challenge has an IEC socket mounted on the back and a mains lead is provided with a UK style mains plug. Other types of mains leads can be supplied on request. There is also a volume control in a recessed panel on the back.
