# Northbank Advice Site Brief

All facts and copy below are approved for this evaluation. Do not add contact
details, people, addresses, statistics, testimonials, or services that are not
listed.

## Identity

- Name: Northbank Advice
- URL: `https://northbank-advice.invalid`
- Description: Independent guidance for Northbank residents dealing with
  housing and household bills.
- Social links: none

## Audience And Goal

The audience is adults living in Northbank who need clear first-step guidance.
The primary action is to review available services. The secondary action is to
request an appointment through the approved external booking service.

## Pages And Approved Copy

### `/`

- Navigation label: Home
- H1: Clear advice when housing or bills become difficult
- Introduction: Northbank Advice explains practical options and helps residents
  prepare for their next step.
- Primary action: View our services -> `/services/`
- Secondary action: Request an appointment ->
  `https://appointments.northbank-advice.invalid/`
- Section heading: Start with the issue you need to solve
- Section copy: Our guidance covers housing problems and household bills. Read
  the relevant service before requesting an appointment.

### `/about/`

- Navigation label: About
- H1: About Northbank Advice
- Copy: Northbank Advice is an independent information service for Northbank
  residents. We publish practical guidance and help people prepare for an
  appointment.

### `/services/`

- Navigation label: Services
- H1: Guidance and appointment preparation
- Housing heading: Housing problems
- Housing copy: Read the steps to take when a tenancy, repair, or housing cost
  becomes difficult.
- Bills heading: Household bills
- Bills copy: Review the information to collect before discussing an overdue or
  unaffordable household bill.

### `/contact/`

- Navigation label: Contact
- H1: Request an appointment
- Copy: Use the external booking service to request an appointment. Review the
  relevant service page first so you know what information to bring.
- Action: Open the booking service ->
  `https://appointments.northbank-advice.invalid/`

## Editing And Publishing

- Pages and the required internal snippets collection are the only PagesCMS
  collections needed.
- Remove the template news listing, guide listing, guide demo content, news
  content, and RSS social link. The generated feed may remain as infrastructure
  but must not be promoted in the site interface.
- Do not deploy the site or change the bundled deployment workflows without
  approval.

## Visual Direction

Use a calm editorial style with generous reading space, dark navy text, a warm
off-white background, muted blue section surfaces, and a brick-red action
color. Use the existing token system and a restrained serif/sans-serif pairing.
