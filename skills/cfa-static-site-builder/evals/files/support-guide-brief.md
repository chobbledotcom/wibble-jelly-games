## Page 2: Prepare Your Information

- Slug: `prepare-information`
- Subtitle: Gather the details needed for a complete request
- Intro: Collect the information below before opening the request form.
- Sections, in this sequence:
  - Your household: Note the number of adults and children in your household.
  - The essential cost: Record what the cost is, who issued it, and when it is
    due.
  - Supporting information: Gather any document that confirms the cost.
- Add an on-page table of contents for those three sections.
- Download label: Application checklist
- Download source: `application-checklist.txt`

## Page 3: Submit A Request

- Slug: `submit-request`
- Subtitle: Review the request before sending it
- Copy: Check every answer and attachment before submitting. Keep the
  confirmation message for your records.
- FAQs maintained as page-level FAQ data:
  - Question: Can I save an unfinished request?
  - Answer: The current request form must be completed in one session.
  - Question: What happens after submission?
  - Answer: You will receive a confirmation message explaining the next step.
